# OTAKU//NEXUS · v0.2 (improved)

Open `index.html` in any modern desktop or mobile browser — fully offline, no install, no account, no payments. Or play online: https://tripletank.github.io/otaku-nexus/

## Play
1. **Home** – claim the daily grant (streak bonus, refills energy).
2. **Summon** – pick a department banner; 1 kit = 100 chips, 10 kits = 900 with an SR+ model guaranteed. Each kit rolls 6 parts with independent rarities.
3. **Workshop** – pick a part per slot (or Auto-pick best) and watch the boot sequence.
4. **Squad** – level, trust and stability; activities (café shift, stage show, diagnostics, stargazing) spend energy. Malfunctioning units (spiral eyes) need repair.
5. **Arena** – turn-based fights (Strike / Combo / Guard / Overclock) across 5 ranks with bosses.
6. **Story** – 4 branching chapters, 3 endings, gated by progress.
7. **Catalog / Exchange / Data** – unit gallery, recycle parts into shards and forge chosen parts, export/import save, achievements, reduce-motion toggle.

## Rates (per slot)
R 70% · SR 21% · SSR 7.5% · UR 1.5%. Model slot: UR odds rise after 50 kits, guaranteed at 70.

## Files
- `index.html`, `game.js` – the game. `assets/*.js` – catalog + embedded portraits (works from file://).
- `data/catalog.json` – editable roster/rates (rebuild `assets/catalog.js` from it, or edit both).
- `img/` – full-size portraits and banners. `renpy/starter.rpy` – Ren'Py proof of concept. `WORLD_BIBLE.md` – lore.

All characters are original adult androids (AI-generated art). Chips and shards are fictional and never sold.
